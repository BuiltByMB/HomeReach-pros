Frequently asked questions

A Few Helpful Answers

Learn how the HomeReach Pros connection process works.

What services can I find on HomeReach Pros?

You can explore plumbing, HVAC, pest control, electrical, and residential locksmith service categories.

How do I contact a service provider?

Choose a service and use its call button when an active phone number has been configured. The phone recipient can explain service coverage, availability, and next steps.

Is there a separate number for each service?

The website is designed for a separate tracking number for each of the five categories. A button will become a working call link only after its real number is added to the website configuration.

Which locations do you serve?

Initial targeting is intended for the United States. Actual coverage depends on the provider or campaign connected to each number. No specific city or ZIP code coverage is represented here unless it is confirmed and added.

Does HomeReach Pros provide the actual repair services?

No. HomeReach Pros is a discovery and connection platform, not the repair contractor. Services, quotes, appointments, and work are handled by the independent provider or campaign recipient you reach.

Are emergency services available?

Some providers may offer emergency services, but availability is not guaranteed. Ask the provider directly whether it handles your request and location at the time you call. If there is an immediate threat to life or safety, contact local emergency services.

Does calling cost anything?

Your phone carrier may apply its normal calling charges. Ask the provider about any inspection, dispatch, service, or other fees before agreeing to work.
